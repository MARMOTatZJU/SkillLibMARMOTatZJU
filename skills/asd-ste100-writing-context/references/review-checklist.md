# ASD-STE100-informed review checklist

## Meaning and terms

Examples: review `valve` versus `component`; verify `port 443`; define `load balancer` at first use.

- [ ] Does every key word have the intended meaning and part of speech?
- [ ] Is each component or action called by one term throughout?
- [ ] Are technical terms necessary, recognized, and defined when unfamiliar?
- [ ] Did the rewrite preserve values, identifiers, conditions, distinctions, and safety information?

## Procedures and safety

Examples: `If the pump is running, turn it off.`; `Remove the four screws.`; `WARNING: Disconnect power. It can cause injury.`

- [ ] Does each step use an imperative and state one action?
- [ ] Is every required condition before the command?
- [ ] Is each procedure sentence within 20 words under the standard count?
- [ ] Do notes contain information only?
- [ ] Does each warning or caution appear before the guarded step and state the consequence?

## Descriptions and structure

Examples: describe the system before its parts; keep startup in one paragraph; split a long explanation into a vertical list.

- [ ] Does the explanation move from general to specific?
- [ ] Does each paragraph have one topic and six sentences or fewer?
- [ ] Is each descriptive sentence within 25 words?
- [ ] Are lists vertical, parallel, and easy to scan?
- [ ] Are cause, order, contrast, and result explicit?

## Grammar and surface form

Examples: `Measure the voltage.`; `Do not use`, not `don't use`; replace `take off` with `remove` when that is the intended meaning.

- [ ] Is the actor visible, with active voice where the agent is known?
- [ ] Are direct verbs used instead of action nouns?
- [ ] Are unnecessary progressive, perfect, and auxiliary constructions removed?
- [ ] Are articles, demonstratives, and pronouns unambiguous?
- [ ] Are semicolons, unclear phrasal verbs, Latin abbreviations, and vague possessives reviewed?
- [ ] Is terminology and spelling consistent?

## Final review

Examples: ask an engineer to check a torque value; ask a safety reviewer to check a warning; label unchecked dictionary status as pending.

- [ ] Can the reader act without asking the author what the sentence means?
- [ ] Did a domain reviewer confirm that the rewrite preserves technical meaning?
- [ ] Is dictionary or certification status reported separately from readability?
