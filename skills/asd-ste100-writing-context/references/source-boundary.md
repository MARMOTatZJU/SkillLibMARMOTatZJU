# Self-contained source boundary

## Included knowledge

This skill bundles a compact paraphrase of the essential ASD-STE100 Issue 9 controls, rule IDs, examples, and review checklist. It is sufficient for an STE-informed readability pass without files outside this folder.

The bundled rule coverage includes:

- Rules 1.1–1.14: words, meanings, parts of speech, technical nouns, technical verbs, and spelling.
- Rules 2.1–2.2: multi-word nouns.
- Rules 3.1–3.7: verb forms, “-ing” forms, voice, and direct verbs.
- Rules 4.1–4.5: sentence completeness, lists, connectors, articles, and demonstratives.
- Rules 5.1–5.5: procedure length, one instruction per sentence, imperative form, conditions, and notes.
- Rules 6.1–6.6: descriptive order, key terms, sentence length, and paragraph structure.
- Rules 7.1–7.3: safety signal, command or condition, hazard, and consequence.
- Rules 8.1–8.7: punctuation and word-count conventions.
- Rules 9.1–9.4: rewriting, correct word use, phrasal verbs, and consistency.
- GR-1–GR-8: conjunctions, prepositions, pronouns, false friends, Latin abbreviations, inclusive language, and possessives.

## Verification boundary

- Treat the bundled content as a practical paraphrase, not a reproduction of the standard.
- Do not reproduce or invent the controlled dictionary.
- Do not claim ASD-STE100 compliance, certification, or dictionary approval from this skill alone.
- Mark dictionary status, exact source wording, and page-specific claims as `pending` unless a human has supplied and checked the authoritative Issue 9 source.
- Preserve project terminology, regulated names, quoted interface text, values, identifiers, and safety requirements even when they fall outside the compact examples.

## Self-contained operating rule

If a task asks for an exact quotation, exact page reference, dictionary lookup, or certification decision, state that the bundled skill cannot establish it and request authoritative source material or human review. Continue with the readability guidance that can be supported by the bundled records.

## Provenance

The content was derived from an essential ASD-STE100 Issue 9 database and its associated writing-rule synthesis. The skill intentionally stores the usable paraphrases and examples locally so that operation does not depend on the original workspace layout.
